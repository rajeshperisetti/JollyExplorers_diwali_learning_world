Lab 04 • Circuit arrangements

🔗 Series and parallel diyas

Understand two ways Diwali diyas or lights can be connected.

DISCOVER IT

Series circuit

Components share one path. The same current passes through each component. One break can stop the whole string.

Parallel circuit

Components have separate branches across the supply. One branch can fail while other branches remain on, if designed correctly.

⏱️ Flashing lights and RGB modes

A tiny controller switches LEDs ON and OFF in patterns. Pick a mode!

DISCOVER IT

A flashing string repeats ON and OFF steps. A timer chip or microcontroller sets the speed and the order. RGB LEDs can also mix red, green and blue to change colour.

Duty cycle = ON time ÷ total time × 100%. 1 second ON and 3 seconds OFF is 25%.

Lab 4 of 5

🌀 Motor chakri

A chakri spins when a small electric motor turns it. Let’s control the spin!

How does a motor work?

Inside the motor, electricity flows through a coil that sits between magnets. The magnets push the coil, so the shaft turns. A stronger push means faster spinning.

Speed control (PWM): a controller switches the power on and off very fast. The longer it stays “on”, the faster the motor spins.

Glowing rings: when LEDs spin fast, your eyes keep each flash for a moment, so the LEDs blur into a glowing circle. This is called persistence of vision.

Real motors can hurt fingers. Build with an adult and keep hands away from spinning parts.
